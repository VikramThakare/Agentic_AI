# Sepsis Deterioration Protocol

**Source**: Educational Prototype Guidelines
**Condition**: Suspected infection with concurrent changes in vital signs (e.g., increased Heart Rate, increased Respiratory Rate, decreased Blood Pressure, or abnormal Temperature).

## Assessment
- Evaluate for signs of systemic infection (fever, chills, altered mental status).
- Review recent lab results for elevated white blood cell count (WBC) or serum lactate.
- Assess perfusion (capillary refill, skin mottling).
- Calculate qSOFA or SIRS criteria if applicable.

## Action Plan
- Notify the attending physician and Rapid Response Team immediately.
- Obtain blood cultures and other appropriate cultures before starting antibiotics.
- Initiate empiric broad-spectrum intravenous (IV) antibiotics within 1 hour.
- Administer IV fluid resuscitation (e.g., 30 mL/kg of crystalloid) for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
- Continuously monitor hemodynamics (Heart Rate and Blood Pressure).
