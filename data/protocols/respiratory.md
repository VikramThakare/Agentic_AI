# Respiratory Deterioration Protocol

**Source**: Educational Prototype Guidelines
**Condition**: Concurrent drop in SpO2 and increase in Respiratory Rate.

## Assessment
- Evaluate airway patency and work of breathing.
- Check for cyanosis, accessory muscle use, or altered mental status.
- Confirm SpO2 readings with an alternative pulse oximeter if signal is poor.

## Action Plan
- Elevate the head of the bed to optimize ventilation.
- Initiate supplemental oxygen therapy (e.g., nasal cannula or simple face mask) to maintain SpO2 above 92% (or patient's baseline).
- Prepare for potential respiratory support (e.g., HFNC, BiPAP, or intubation) if no improvement.
- Notify the attending physician or rapid response team immediately.
