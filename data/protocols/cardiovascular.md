# Cardiovascular Deterioration Protocol

**Source**: Educational Prototype Guidelines
**Condition**: Sudden changes in Heart Rate combined with hypotension (low blood pressure) or hypertension.

## Assessment
- Assess peripheral perfusion, capillary refill time, and skin temperature.
- Evaluate the patient for chest pain, dizziness, palpitations, or altered mental status.
- Obtain an immediate 12-lead ECG.

## Action Plan
- Ensure patent IV access is available.
- If hypotensive, consider laying the patient flat and prepare for potential fluid resuscitation as directed by a physician.
- If tachycardic and unstable, prepare for immediate medical intervention or synchronized cardioversion.
- Notify the attending physician or rapid response team immediately.
